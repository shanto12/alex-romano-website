The Journey

From Construction Sites to Boardrooms

Alexander "Alex" Romano's story is the embodiment of the American Dream. Born into modest circumstances, Alex learned the value of hard work early in life. At just 16 years old, he was working construction sites after school, developing an intimate understanding of how buildings come together from the ground up.

This hands-on experience became his greatest asset. While others saw buildings, Alex saw opportunities. He studied every aspect of real estate development, from architecture to finance, spending his nights learning what he couldn't learn during the day.

Today, as the CEO of Romano Group, Alex oversees a portfolio worth billions, with landmark properties across 12 countries. But his hunger for excellence and his commitment to creating architectural masterpieces that stand the test of time remains unchanged.
