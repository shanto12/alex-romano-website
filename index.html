Story of Ascension

From Miami Streets to Global Skylines

Born to immigrant parents in Miami, Alexander Romano learned the value of perseverance stocking shelves in his family's corner store. At 17, he was flipping abandoned duplexes before dawn, reinvesting every dollar into his dream of designing landmarks that outlive generations.

Two decades later, Alex is the Founder & CEO of Romano Group, commanding a $2.8B empire redefining the luxury skyline across Miami, New York City, Dubai, Monaco, and beyond. His approach merges precision engineering with couture hospitality, ensuring every development is an immersive experience curated for global elites.

From private heliports to bespoke fragrance programs, Alex's properties are more than structures—they are statements of power, legacy, and arrival.
